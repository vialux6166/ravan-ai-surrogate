Core Theory: Quantum Mechanics Fundamentals
The simulations implement three key quantum mechanical frameworks:

Quantum Circuit Theory (Qiskit-based)

Formula: |ψ⟩ = U |ψ₀⟩, where U is unitary gate operations
Variables: Qubit states |0⟩, |1⟩, superposition coefficients, measurement probabilities
Key Principle: Entanglement and superposition enable parallel computation
Time-Dependent Schrödinger Equation

Formula: iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = H ψ, where H = T + V (kinetic + potential energy)
Variables:
Wavefunction ψ(x,t)
Position x, momentum p, energy E
Potential V(x), initial conditions (x₀, k₀, σ)
Key Principle: Wave-particle duality, quantum tunneling, uncertainty principle
Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

Formula: H = p²/2m + ½mω²x², eigenvalues Eₙ = (n + ½)ℏω
Variables: Oscillator length a, basis size N, time t, photon number n
Key Principle: Quantized energy levels, coherent state evolution
